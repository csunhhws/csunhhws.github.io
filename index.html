EXCESSIVE HEAT WARNING

103°F expected — dangerous heat in effect for Los Angeles County

An Excessive Heat Warning means a prolonged period of dangerously hot temperatures. Heat-related illness risk is high for everyone, especially older adults, young children, outdoor workers, and people without reliable air conditioning.

  • 💧 Drink water every 15–20 minutes, even if you don't feel thirsty.
  • 🏠 Move to an air-conditioned space during the hottest hours, 12–6 PM.
  • 🚗 Never leave children, pets, or medications in a parked car.
  • 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Check on older neighbors and relatives at least twice a day.
  • 🚑 Call 911 for confusion, hot dry skin, or fainting — signs of heat stroke.

02 · Heat & Health

Know the signs. Beat the heat.

Extreme heat is the leading weather-related cause of death in the United States. Most heat illness is preventable once you know what to watch for.

Mild

Heat Cramps

Watch for: Painful muscle spasms, usually in the legs or abdomen, during or after exertion.

Do: Stop activity, move to shade, sip water or a sports drink, gently stretch the muscle.

Moderate

Heat Exhaustion

Watch for: Heavy sweating, cold clammy skin, dizziness, nausea, headache, weakness.

Do: Move to a cool place, loosen clothing, sip water, apply cool wet cloths. Seek care if symptoms worsen or last over an hour.

Severe · Emergency

Heat Stroke

Watch for: Body temp above 103°F, hot red dry or damp skin, confusion, rapid pulse, possible loss of consciousness.

Do: Call 911 immediately. Move to shade, cool the body with water or ice while waiting for help. Do not give fluids by mouth.

Ways to stay cool

🥤 Drink water often — don't wait to feel thirsty
🧴 Wear light, loose, light-colored clothing
🕶️ Limit outdoor activity 12–6 PM
🏢 Visit a cooling center or air-conditioned space
🚿 Take cool showers or use damp towels
🐾 Never leave people or pets in parked cars
🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Check in on elderly neighbors and relatives
🍽️ Eat light meals — skip heavy, hot food
